Short rounds

Up to 10 random words per session, drawn from a collection of 710 everyday words.

Practice together

12 sound choices, spoken examples, and a parent check after each recognized word.

On your device

Practice audio stays on the device. No saved recordings, advertising, or tracking.

How a round works

  1. Choose an animal and one or more sounds.
  2. Say the word while the microphone indicator is on.
  3. A parent checks the pronunciation, then celebrates or tries again.
  4. Finish the round, or skip a word and take a break whenever you like.

Practice together with a parent or caregiver. Speech Practice is an educational practice tool, not a diagnosis, clinical assessment, or replacement for advice from a qualified speech-language professional. The device recognizes words; a parent decides whether the pronunciation was clear.

For iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. English (US) speech recognition availability varies by device and installed system resources.
