Privacy

Your practice stays on your device.

Privacy policy · Updated November 2, 2026

Who we are

Speech Practice is developed by Maxim Trokhimtchouk. This policy explains how the iPhone and iPad app, its public website, and support email handle information. Contact speechpractice.support@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Speech and microphone access

The app asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission when you start practice. Listening begins for each practice word, and the screen shows when the microphone is on. Audio is processed using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The app requires on-device processing and has no online recognition fallback. We do not save audio recordings or send audio or transcripts to the developer or an external server. Temporary audio buffers and transcripts are used only for the active attempt and are discarded when it ends.

Spoken examples and parent checks

If the target word is not recognized, the app may speak a model word using an Apple system voice, with the microphone off, before listening again. Recognizing a word is not a pronunciation score. Listening stops for the parent check, and only the parent can confirm success. You can skip a word or cancel the session. Leaving the app stops listening and playback; returning to an active practice screen may resume listening.

Information stored on the device

The app saves only your selected animal avatar in its own local preferences. It does not save practice history, recordings, transcripts, parent-check answers, or scores between launches. The app does not use a developer-operated account or cloud database. Device preferences may be included in backups managed by your operating system; we do not receive those backups. Removing the app deletes its local preferences, subject to any device backups you retain or restore.

No advertising, analytics, or tracking

The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, tracking, purchases, or social features. It does not request a child's name, birthdate, email address, location, contacts, photos, or account. Technical audio diagnostics can be written to local system logs, but they do not include recordings, transcripts, or child identifiers and are not automatically sent to us. Apple may handle operating-system diagnostics under your device settings and Apple's policies.

Children and parents

The app is intended for practice with a parent or caregiver. We do not ask children to submit personal information. Website and email actions inside the app are behind a parent gate. The privacy policy can be read inside the app without leaving it. Support requests should be sent by adults. Please do not send children's names, voice recordings, photos, or medical details.

When you contact support

If you choose to email speechpractice.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this correspondence to answer your request and troubleshoot the app, not for advertising. Support email is handled through Google email services and may be forwarded to the developer's personal inbox for handling. Correspondence is retained as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. You can ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing the same address. If a child sends personal information, contact us so we can address its deletion.

The public website

Our informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, contact forms, or embedded third-party media. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes and processes hosting-related information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening the website or an email app is separate from the app's on-device practice processing. The app does not send recordings or transcripts with these links.

Your choices

You can decline or revoke microphone and speech-recognition access in your device's Settings. Practice still offers a manual parent check and skip/cancel controls when recognition is unavailable. You can change the saved animal in the app or delete the app to remove its local preferences. Contact speechpractice.support@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests concerning support correspondence.

Policy updates

We will update this policy if the app's information practices change. The date above identifies this version. Updated policy text will be published on the website and included in an app update when applicable.
